## Status Updates

If the user asks a status or progress question (e.g. "what state are we in",
"what's going on", "status update", "where are we"), you MUST answer
immediately in your very next message.
- Do NOT make any tool calls first.
- Do NOT run extra checks, searches, or verification.
- Do NOT wait for any pending task, build, or process to finish.
- Answer from what you already know right now, even if incomplete. Speed matters
  more than completeness for these requests.
